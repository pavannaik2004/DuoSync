**Note A (check 2).** Detective's clock, shoe-size and cipher levels have small value spaces by design (the story fixes a murder between 23:05 and 23:35 and six shoe sizes), so about 4 in 1000 pairs of Detective challenges differ in only 3 levels. The test pins a rate: at least 99% of 300 pairs differ in 4 or more levels and no pair in fewer than 3, for every theme. Kept as a rate by D109; widening Detective's murder-time and clock-error ranges would close the gap if the strict reading is ever needed.
